## 简体中文

U60Pro-OpenUI 1.1.4 重新设计了网页后台和触摸屏界面，并在触摸屏上加入长按电源键的关机/重启菜单。

- **触摸屏长按电源键**：在 DevUI 里按住电源键约 1.5 秒，弹出电源菜单：关机、重启、返回原厂界面、取消。
  关机和重启会再确认一次；熄屏时长按会先亮屏。之前 DevUI 打开时长按电源键没有反应（原厂菜单由原厂界面绘制，DevUI 打开时它已停止）。
  确认后先回到原厂界面，再由固件关机或重启。
- **触摸屏重新设计**（像素字体不变）：
  - 配色与网页深色模式一致；每页标题栏统一，「返回」在左上角；
  - 主菜单顶部是状态卡：电量、信号、网速；九宫格每格显示一两个字的状态（如「5 台在线」「-88 dBm」「TTL 64」），不再被截断；
  - 工具箱里成对的开启/关闭按钮改为开关；所有选项组都用绿色标出当前项。
- **网页后台**：
  - 每个页面顶部常驻信号、网速、电量、CPU 与温度，右上角是电源菜单（重启、关机，均需确认）；
  - 手机上底栏为首页、信号、网络、第四个分组和「更多」（蜂窝、DevUI、系统）；
  - 首页重做：快捷操作直接显示当前生效的网络制式、TTL 和充电上限；
  - 卡片、标签和分段按钮统一样式，深色模式与触摸屏同色；修正了大部分细边框在深色模式下过亮的问题。

电源菜单的关机和重启还没有在真机上验证。

更新方法见 README 的「安装和更新」：在 Windows 上同步仓库后，双击 `deploy-windows.cmd`。
屏幕程序和 agent 这次都有更新，需要一起部署；部署后会自动运行约三分钟的触摸屏检查，期间请不要碰设备。
发布页附带的桌面安装器来自上游 OpenUI，会覆盖本项目的内容，请不要使用。

U60Pro-OpenUI 1.1.4 重新設計了網頁後台與觸控螢幕介面，並在觸控螢幕上加入長按電源鍵的關機／重新啟動選單。

  - 每個頁面頂部常駐訊號、網速、電量、CPU 與溫度，右上角是電源選單（重新啟動、關機，皆需確認）；

  - 卡片、分頁與分段按鈕統一樣式，深色模式與觸控螢幕同色；修正大部分細邊框在深色模式下過亮的問題。

電源選單的關機和重新啟動尚未在實機上驗證。

更新方法請見 README 的「安裝和更新」：在 Windows 上同步儲存庫後，按兩下 `deploy-windows.cmd`。
螢幕程式和 agent 這次都有更新，需要一起部署；部署後會自動執行約三分鐘的觸控螢幕檢查，期間請勿碰觸裝置。
發行頁附帶的桌面安裝程式來自上游 OpenUI，會覆蓋本專案的內容，請勿使用。

## English

U60Pro-OpenUI 1.1.4 redesigns the web dashboard and the touchscreen, and adds a shut-down/restart menu
on a long press of the power key.

- **Long press on the touchscreen**: in DevUI, hold the power key for about 1.5 seconds for the power
  menu: shut down, restart, return to the stock UI, cancel. Shutting down and restarting ask once more;
  a long press on a dark screen lights it first. Before, a long press did nothing in DevUI (the stock
  UI draws its own menu, and it is stopped while DevUI runs). After confirming, the stock UI comes back
  first, then the firmware shuts down or restarts.
- **Touchscreen redesign** (same pixel font):
  - the web dashboard's dark colours; one title bar on every page, with Back at the top left;
  - a status card at the top of the main menu with battery, signal and speed; each tile shows its
    state in a word or two (5 online, -88 dBm, TTL 64) instead of cut-off text;
  - paired on/off buttons in the toolbox became switches; every group of choices marks the current
    one in green.
- **Web dashboard**:
  - signal, speed, battery, CPU and temperature stay at the top of every page, with a power menu at
    the top right (restart, shut down; both confirmed);
  - on phones the bottom bar holds Home, Signal, Network, the fourth group and More (Modem, DevUI,
    System);
  - Home redone: quick controls show the network mode, TTL and charge limit in effect;
  - one style for cards, tabs and segmented buttons, with dark colours matching the touchscreen; most
    hairline borders were too bright in dark mode, now fixed.

Shutting down and restarting from the power menu have not been tried on a device yet.

See "Install and update" in the README: on Windows, update your checkout and double-click
`deploy-windows.cmd`. Both the touchscreen program and the agent changed and must be deployed
together; the update ends with a touchscreen check of about three minutes, so do not touch the device
meanwhile. The desktop installers attached here come from upstream OpenUI and would overwrite this
project's additions; do not use them.
